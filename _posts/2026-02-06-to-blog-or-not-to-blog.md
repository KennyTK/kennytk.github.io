# To blog or not to blog

## That is the question

I have thrown around the idea of blogging for a few years but I've failed to find a reason to start. I'm young and don't have much to give in regards to programming experience. Once or twice, I've come across issues such as winforms scroll bars that could warrant a blog post but those are few and far between.
  
In addition, I don’t really like writing. I'm not particularly outstanding at it. However, I've found myself down many rabbitholes that end in blog posts and I'm grateful for the people who blog. Hopefully through this blog I will be able to give back to the community. In the moment, writing is dull but I do enjoy reading my previous work. 
  
To my future self-- *be happy that you wrote this*. To the unexpected reader who stumbles across this-- ```hello world```.
